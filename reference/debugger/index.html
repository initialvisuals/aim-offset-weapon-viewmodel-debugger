    Settings
    
        
      
    
      
    Press O anytime to open or close. Gameplay pauses while open.
    

      
    
        
    Game style
    
        
    
          
          
        
    
        
    Sim = HoB + ballistic zero. Arcade = reticle-faithful (bore = camera aim).
    
      
    

      
    
        
    Display
    
        
        
        
        
        
    1.00 / 1.00 is ungraded sRGB. Scene time-of-day owns the look; use the sliders if your panel is dim.

    White
    
        
    
        
        
    If Black blends into Low, your display is crushing shadows — raise Brightness/Gamma.
    
      
    

      
    
        
    FX
    
        
        
        
        
        
    Env / silhouette holes FIFO-cap and optionally expire. Paper-target holes stay until table reset. Berm popups still clear on hide. Casings sleep, then expire (0 = until the cap recycles). Lowering a cap trims oldest immediately.
    
      
    

      
    
        
    Lighting
    
        
        
        
    Scene sun, sky, and fog. Display brightness/gamma stay as grade. Default evening keeps the dusk range; night stays dark (stars, not a washed navy). Clouds 0 = clear. Stars follow the clock. Intensity sliders multiply the current clock (1.00 = authored ToD).
    
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
    Volumetric sun shafts from the ToD sun (shadow-map ray march). Strongest at low sun looking toward the light; noon is milder; night is off. 0 = skip the pass.
    
        
        
    HDR glow on floods, muzzle, sun, and tracers. Walls and floor stay put. Independent of god rays. 0 = skip the pass.
    
      
    

      
    
        
    Materials
    
        
        
    Dust, edge wear, and base grime on bay walls, floor, and berm. 0 = clean pour. Default dusty indoor. Benches stay wood.
    
      
    

      
    
        
    Fog
    
        
        
        
        
    Linear distance fog (THREE.Fog). Color tracks clear/background. Density (FogExp2) not exposed yet.
    
      
    

      
    
        
    Camera clip
    
        
    Near/far planes for depth teaching. Defaults clear the ~410 m berm; logarithmic depth is on.
    
        
        
      
    

      
    
        
    Ballistics
    
        
        
    Zero distance mainly applies in Sim.
